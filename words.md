# Words

**Rooter.** The protocol, and the project that builds it.

**ROOTR.** The short name. `$ROOTR` is the token's ticker.

**Root.** To launch a coin for someone, hold it, or bring someone else to it. "ROOT someone" is the button.

**A rooter.** Anyone who roots. Lower case, to tell the person from the protocol.

**The person.** The account a coin is keyed to, and the human behind it.

**Launcher.** The wallet that birthed the coin.

**Quote.** What a coin trades against and pays in: ETH, or an allowed Robinhood Stock Token.

**Escrow.** The person's share, held under their account until they claim it.

**Claim.** Proving a handle to unlock its escrow.

**Sweep.** After 90 days unclaimed, paying the escrow to the coin's holders.

**Rooter Pool.** The per-coin pool that pays holders by balance and time.

**Finder's fee.** The launcher's share, paid once the person has claimed.

**Root link.** A referral link. Pays on each trade it brings.

**Matchup.** Two coins, seven days, one prize.

**Stock Token.** An ERC-20 issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited that tracks a US share or ETF.

**$ROOTR.** The protocol's fixed-supply token. Launches first, on Robinhood Chain.

**Robinhood Chain.** An Ethereum Layer 2 built by Robinhood, chain id 4663, where all of this runs.
