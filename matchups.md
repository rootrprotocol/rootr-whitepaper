# Matchups

Who are you rooting for? A matchup makes it a real question with a scoreboard.

## The rules

- Two coins. Seven days.
- A sponsor puts up a prize in ETH or $ROOTR. Anyone can sponsor. ROOTR sponsors one featured matchup each week from its own share of fees.
- Rooted volume on each coin during the window is counted from the fee hook's own events.
- At the close, the prize is paid into the winning coin's Rooter Pool and distributed to its holders like any other fee.

## What a matchup changes

Only the prize. The fee, the split, the escrow and the pools are untouched. A matchup is a bonus on top of rooting, not a different way to root. Losing costs a rooter nothing beyond the trading they chose to do.

## What a matchup cannot do

It cannot be settled by hand. Volume is read from the chain, the winner is whichever number is larger, and the prize goes only to the winner's Rooter Pool. It cannot be cancelled once funded. A tie splits the prize.

Matchups are a later phase of the protocol; see [What comes next](what-comes-next.md).
