# What comes next

Rooter ships in an order, not on a calendar. Each step starts when the one before it has been checked. No step below has a date, and none will be given one until it is done.

**1. This document and the token.** This document, the site, and $ROOTR on Robinhood Chain. The token address, supply and treasury addresses go on the Addresses page.

**2. The build.** The contracts in [Under the hood](under-the-hood.md) are written and tested against a copy of Robinhood Chain and on its public testnet. An outside audit of the fee hook, the escrow, the Rooter Pool and the liquidity lock, with the full report published. Deployment behind the pause, exercised with the team's own money on real coins, every contract verified on the explorer, every address published.

**3. Rooting opens.** ETH-quoted coins for everyone. The Rooter Pool, the finder's fee, root links and sweeps are live from the first coin. Every fee, claim, sweep and payout is on the chain, and a public log shows them.

**4. Root in their stock.** The allowed list opens with the first Robinhood Stock Tokens, with the eligibility rules in [Root in their stock](root-in-their-stock.md) applied in the interface.

**5. Matchups and rooter weight.** Sponsored matchups and the weekly featured one. $ROOTR staking for Rooter Pool weight, written up on its own and audited before it ships.

Three things are not on this list and never will be: a way to withdraw locked liquidity, a way to reach an account's escrow without its claim, and a fee above three percent.
