You trade against the coin's locked pool. 3% of the quote side is taken before the swap runs. The pool charges no other fee, so 3% is the whole cost.

Half a point of every trade goes to the coin's Rooter Pool, paid by how much you held and for how long. Hold through a quiet month and you earn from every trade in it.

Every wallet has a root link for every coin. A trade through yours pays you a quarter point of it, in that transaction. It comes out of the protocol's share, not the person's or the rooters'.

Root in their stock

Root for the founder.
Pay them in the company.

A coin can be quoted in a Robinhood Stock Token instead of ETH. Fees accrue and are paid in that token, to the person, the launcher, the rooters and the root links alike. The pool trades at any hour. Stock-market hours don't apply.

Stock Tokens are issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited under its own terms. They may not be offered, sold or delivered in the United States, and further restrictions apply in the UK, Canada and Switzerland. The Rooter interface applies those rules to stock-quoted coins. Rooter does not issue, custody, mint, redeem or price Stock Tokens.

Straight talk

What can't happen.
And what can.

Every design has a price. Here is Rooter's, said plainly, so nothing is discovered later.

Can't happen

No withdraw function.Liquidity is locked at birth. Not the launcher, not the person, not the team can take it out.
Escrow is theirs.It moves only on a verified claim by that account, or by a public sweep to that coin's own holders after 90 days.
Never above 3%.The fee can't change on a past trade and can't go higher. Every range is published.
Exits don't pause.Sells, claims, holder withdrawals and sweeps can't be paused. Only new launches and new buys can, in an emergency.

Can happen

A coin can go to zero.Locked liquidity stops a rug. It doesn't mean anyone will buy.
No trades, no pay.Every payment is a slice of a real trade. There is no rate and nothing that tries to produce one.
The person may never come.A coin is a bet on attention, not an endorsement. Every coin shows whether its person has claimed.
Code can be wrong.The contracts are audited before mainnet. Audits find bugs. They don't prove there are none.
